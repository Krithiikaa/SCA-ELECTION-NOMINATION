## The three offices

You may contest for **one** of these. Three seats each, first filed, first served.

| Office | Seats open |
|---|---|
| Vice President | 3 |
| Vice Secretary | 3 |
| Vice Treasurer | 3 |

---

## How to file

1. Open the nomination page — **[link goes here]**
2. Decide which office you want to contest.
3. Under that office, click the search box and start typing your **name or register number**.
4. Select yourself from the list that appears.
5. Click **File Nomination**.
6. Your name now shows under that office's filled seats — for everyone, in real time.

---

## Before you click

- **One office per person.** The moment you file, your name disappears from the other two lists — you can't be picked twice.
- **Only 3 seats per office.** Once an office shows *"All 3 seats filled,"* it's closed.
- **There's no undo.** Pick the office you actually mean to contest.
- If your name doesn't appear in the search, or you hit an error, don't retry blindly — contact us first (below).
